# BLOCKED LOVE

A Creative Computing project exploring love, confinement, and domestic abuse through the metaphor of bees trapped in cages.

## Concept

Bees represent people who should be free to fly, love, and live. The cage symbolizes confinement and the loss of freedom within an abusive relationship. The project reflects on the harm caused by violence and encourages communication instead.
